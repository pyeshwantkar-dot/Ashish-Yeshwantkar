About Me

I am a counselling and training professional with a background in Social Work and Rural Development. My work focuses on listening without judgment, helping people navigate stress, and supporting informed decisions about wellbeing and public health.

Through public health counselling and related work, I have contributed to reaching and supporting more than 10,000 people. I value empathy, confidentiality, respect and responsible guidance.
